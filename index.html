FAQ

What's the story behind my travel blog?

It all started with a one-way ticket and a half-empty backpack. After a spontaneous trip through Portugal, I realized I was taking more notes than photos, and friends kept asking for my tips. So I started writing everything down in a small blog. What began as a personal travel diary has grown into a place for honest stories, practical guides, and a few hidden gems you won't find in every guidebook.

How do I plan, document, and share my travels?

I start with a rough route and leave plenty of room for surprises. On the road I take photos, record short voice notes, and jot down details in a notebook while they're still fresh. Back at my desk, I turn it all into stories and guides, often with a packing list, a budget overview, and tips on getting around. New posts go up here on the blog, and behind-the-scenes moments land on my social channels.

How can brands, companies, or fellow creators collaborate with me or get in touch?

I love working with partners who fit the blog and its readers, such as hotels, tour providers, outdoor gear, and tourism boards. I only recommend what I would use myself, and sponsored content is always labeled. Fellow creators are welcome for guest posts or joint trips. Just send me a short message with your idea at travelblog@example.com, and I'll get back to you within a few days.

How can readers support this blog and the work behind it?

The easiest way is to read, comment, and share posts with friends who love to travel. If you'd like to do more, you can use my affiliate links when booking (it costs you nothing extra) or buy me a coffee to fuel the next trip. Every bit of support helps me keep the blog ad-light and my content free for everyone.
