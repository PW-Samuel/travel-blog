A Stylish Scandinavian Escape

Copenhagen in 3 Days: Big Experiences, Smart Spending

Discover Copenhagen, where historic charm meets modern design, vibrant cycling culture and waterfront living. Experience the heart of Scandinavia through cozy cafés, innovative cuisine, and effortlessly cool city life.

I’ve been traveling since 2017, chasing moments instead of plans. I’m fascinated by snakes, love sharks, would happily spend weeks deep in the jungle, and can never get enough papaya. As long as there’s a next destination, I know I’m exactly where I’m meant to be.

Copenhagen has a way of making you slow down. Colorful harbor houses line the canals, bikes glide past in every direction, and the smell of fresh cinnamon buns drifts out of tiny bakeries on almost every corner. In just three days, I discovered that this city is so much more than its famous Little Mermaid.

I wanted to see how far I could get without spending a fortune, and the answer surprised me. With a city pass, a rented bike and a few local tips, I explored royal palaces, hidden courtyards and the best street food markets in town. Here is everything I learned, so you can plan your own perfect Copenhagen weekend.

Before you pack your bags, here are my best tips for saving money in Copenhagen:

  • Get the Copenhagen Card. It includes free public transport and entry to more than 80 museums and attractions.
  • Rent a bike instead of taking taxis. The city is flat, the bike lanes are everywhere, and it is the fastest way to get around.
  • Eat at street food markets like Reffen. You can try dishes from all over the world for a fraction of restaurant prices.

On my first day, I started early at Nyhavn, when the colorful houses were still quiet and the boats rocked gently in the harbor. From there, I cycled along the water to the Little Mermaid and continued to the royal palace of Amalienborg, just in time for the changing of the guard. In the afternoon, I wandered through the narrow streets of the old town, stopped for a coffee in a tiny café and spent the evening at the Tivoli Gardens. When the lights came on and the music started, the whole park felt like a fairy tale, and I knew this trip was worth every single krone.

  1. Buy your Copenhagen Card online before you arrive, it is often cheaper than at the airport. Useful link. Plan your museum visits on the days with the longest opening hours to get the most out of it.
  2. Visit Nyhavn early in the morning for the best photos without crowds. Instead of an expensive canal tour, take the harbor bus: it follows almost the same route and is included in your Copenhagen Card.
